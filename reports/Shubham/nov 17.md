We also studied a scenario called Upload of Inappropriate Content. This scenario is about stopping users from uploading content that is explicit, violent, or hateful. The main actors in this process are:

User

LLM / Image Processor

Content Filtering System

Administrator

The steps in this scenario are:

The user uploads a scanned page of a book.

The system (LLM or image model) checks the content.

The system finds explicit or hateful material.

The upload is blocked.

The user’s account is flagged for review.

The administrator gets a report with details.

The user receives a warning or suspension depending on how serious the issue is.

Learning this scenario helped us understand how systems can prevent harmful content from being uploaded. Overall, today was productive and helped us improve our understanding of system diagrams and content safety.
