Today, we discussed possible solutions in our group to avoid legal issues when providing copyrighted content to users. We talked about three main ideas:

Create a blacklist of copyrighted books that includes the copyright expiration dates. If a user tries to access a book that is still protected, the system will block access.

Provide the content in parts instead of giving the whole book at once, which may help reduce legal risks by limiting how much copyrighted material is shared at a time.

Host the database in a country with more flexible copyright laws, which could make it easier to store and manage copyrighted content legally.

These ideas helped us explore different ways to handle copyrighted materials while staying within legal boundaries.
